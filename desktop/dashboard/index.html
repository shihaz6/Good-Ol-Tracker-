How to get a Gmail App Password
  1. Turn on 2-Step Verification for your Google account (required for App Passwords).
  2. Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords, name it "Good ol Tracker", click Create.
  3. Copy the 16 letters into the box above and Save. Revoke it there any time.

Emails contain totals, top app/site names, topic words and the AI summary. Never page text, titles, URLs or your searches.
